From campaign brief to organic momentum

Political Campaigns · 2022

Context

Two state deputy campaigns in Minas Gerais via independent agency (Reconceito), Jul–Nov 2022.

Challenge

Accounts had very low engagement, in a context of daily electoral pressure and a sensitive political topic where any tone or timing error had a high cost.

Strategic decision

Multi-platform strategy (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts) with political content adapted to each network's native language instead of reusing generic institutional material — prioritizing daily consistency while maintaining editorial care.

Execution

Daily editorial calendar per candidate, short campaign videos, Meta Ads management with budget optimization and targeting by each client's real objective (leads, reach, community).

Result

+40% follower growth, +225% engagement growth, 2.5M+ organic reach, in 8 months.

Context

Internal communication channel for 25,000+ employees at Telefónica Brasil (Vivo), 2023–May 2025.

Challenge

Editorial calendar aligned with brand strategy and business objectives, with continuous KPI tracking to iteratively adjust content and formats.

Execution

Scripting and producing video for the internal channel, copy for internal portals, executive presentations and training materials, moderation of internal digital channels.

Result

PoC in Dental · US Nonprofit Association · 2026–Present

Context

PoC in Dental (People of Color in Dental), US-based nonprofit professional association, remote contract, Aug 2026–present.

Challenge

Low engagement, stalled newsletter, and no structured sponsor prospecting flow via LinkedIn.

Strategic decision

Revive a weekly editorial calendar integrated with the newsletter and create an active LinkedIn outreach flow to decision makers instead of relying only on passive organic engagement.

Execution

Weekly social/LinkedIn content calendar, newsletter relaunch, pre/post-event communication, direct outreach to members and potential sponsors.

Result

2 meetings booked with decision makers within 2 weeks of starting active prospecting.
